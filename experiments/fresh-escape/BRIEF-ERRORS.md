## Post-run (written by the overnight orchestrating session, after reading the ledger)

- No brief errors: nothing in the PREREG was found unimplementable. One pre-run review finding changed the code, not the contract: the reading of "measured on every seed before the deadline" was made literal (measurements are stamped, and only those completed by the `deadline` count toward `P`), rather than amending the PREREG.
- The deadline did not bind. `post_deadline_measurements` is empty, and `checkpoints_measured` lists every listed checkpoint on every seed, so the pre-run reviewer's note (that `checkpoints_measured` would overstate if the deadline bound) does not arise in this run.
- The outcome matches the most likely case in the frozen `expected` row.
- Evicted-bucket and leak check at `P`: on every seed `A.ckpt9000.heldout.live.gap_gt_M.answer_acc` is exactly equal to `A.ckpt9000.heldout.slots_zeroed.gap_gt_M.answer_acc`, so zeroing the memory changes nothing for queries whose answer has been evicted. In the other buckets the live and zeroed accuracies at `P` differ only by amounts of the size of `A.ckpt9000.R_quantity`, far below `delta`. Both conditions sit at the level of the start checkpoint (compare `A.ckpt3000.*` in runs/fresh-stream/ledger.json); nothing is retrieved through the memory.
- `A.ckpt9000.R_quantity` is small and positive on every seed, and identical across seeds to the rendered precision. It is far below `delta` and is not read as a trend: the earlier checkpoints' `R_quantity` values move both ways around zero.
- The stream answer loss never crossed `stream_loss_threshold` at any step on any seed (`stream_answer_loss_first_window_below` is empty for every seed). Every window stayed on the plateau fresh-stream's arm A reached; see `stream_answer_loss_window_means`.
- The fresh-escape mutations in scripts/mutation_battery.py were proven by hand against tests/test_fresh_escape.py only, not by the full battery. The full battery run on main the same night (at the merge base, without these mutations) found five older gates whose mutations redden undeclared tests in tests/test_fresh_stream.py and tests/test_scaffold_dose.py. Whether the new fresh-escape mutations leak the same way is unknown until the next full battery run.
