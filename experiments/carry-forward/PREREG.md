## Instrument

`loo_readout(model, docs, ids, mask, tmask, gap, sym_ids, seed=<seed>)` from
`src/rsr/metrics/loo.py` (W4). Settings:
- FIFO, eval mode, no grad.
- The model is S0-03's `_cfg(V)` with the vocabulary of documents `[0, 64)`, loaded with
  `rsr.train.checkpoint.load(restore_rng=False)`, on CPU at `torch.set_num_threads(12)`. That is the
  fresh-stream parent's thread count (its ledger's `parent_torch_threads`).
- The donor seed is the seed number.

### Known limitations of the instrument, and how each is handled

1. **`all_slots_zeroed` is single-step.** Memory is zeroed for the query's forward only, on the live
   trajectory. S0-03's `slots_zeroed` zeroes every step, so the bos-copy context is also changed. The
   two are not interchangeable.
   - Every number that is compared with S0-03 or with the fresh-stream ledger uses `answer_readout`.
   - L's denominator is the single-step zeroing, so the numerator and denominator are the same kind
     of intervention.
2. **The control target is often missing, and not at random.** It exists only when an adjacent rank
   holds a still-pending assert of another fact.
   - S is computed only on targets with `ctrl_status == 0` and both resample donors present.
   - Its coverage is reported as a fraction of the L population.
   - L is also reported restricted to the S population, so any conditioning bias is visible.
   - Below 0.20 coverage, S is `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE`.
3. **Donors come only from the batch passed in.**
   - H64 is one call of 64 rows.
   - EXT is four calls of 256 consecutive ids (`[64,320)`, `[320,576)`, `[576,832)`, `[832,1088)`).
     Each row's donor pool is the other 255 rows of its call, at the same step and rank.
   - `own_resample` coverage below 0.80 of the gap 2..M targets makes the run inconclusive for L.
4. **FIFO only.** This measures FIFO memory, which is what arm B trained with. Nothing here is about
   an RSR policy.

## Sets

- **H64** = `[4096, 4160)`, via `experiments/corpus-size-curve/run.py::doc_sets(seed, 64)`. This is
  the path fresh-stream measured. S0-03's manifest text `generate(n=128)[64:]` does not describe
  this set.
- **EXT** = `[64, 1088)`, 1024 documents per seed. They are generated by id (prefix-stable: seed

- **Disjointness.** Arm B's lineage trained on `[0, 64)` (corpus-size n64, steps 0–999) and then on
  the stream `[4160, 52160)` (steps 1000–2999). Fresh-escape's stream is `[52160, 148160)`. The
  vocabulary/probe set is `[0, 64)` and H64 is `[4096, 4160)`. EXT must be disjoint from every one of
  these, and must have no repeated id.

## Controls (checked first; each overrides everything)

1. **Checkpoint identity.** Each of the six checkpoints exists and stores the step it is measured
   as. Its sha256 is recorded. Nothing under `.worktrees/fresh-stream/` is opened for writing.
2. **Reproduction.** On H64, per seed and checkpoint, S0-03's `answer_readout` under `live` and
   `slots_zeroed`, summarised by `_summarise`, must equal fresh-stream's
   `raw.json arms.B.per.<s>.<c>.s003.heldout.{live,slots_zeroed}`:
   - every bucket, every statistic, and `real_token_nll`, within 1e-6;
   - every `n`, exactly.

   At ckpt 3000 the per-seed values must also equal the samples of the ledger's
   `B.ckpt3000.heldout.{live,slots_zeroed}.*` rows.
3. **LOO live is `answer_readout` live, bit-exact.** On every `loo_readout` call (H64 and each EXT
   chunk, every seed and checkpoint), `live_nll`, `live_ok`, `live_nll16` and `live_brier16` equal
   `answer_readout(cond="live")` on the same batch, under `torch.equal`, target for target.
4. **EXT disjointness and vocabulary closure.**
   - EXT is disjoint from and repeats nothing in the ranges listed under Sets.
   - `rsr.train.loop.check_vocabulary_closure(EXT docs, vocab[0,64))` raises nothing.
   - H64 generated by id equals `doc_sets`' H64, as a prefix-stability check.
5. **FIFO residency.** For every target, `own_resident == (gap <= M)` with M = 16.
6. **Resample coverage.** For every seed and checkpoint, on H64, at least 0.80 of the gap 2..M
   targets have `own_resample` applied.

## Readouts

All readouts are per seed × checkpoint in {2500, 3000}. Accuracy is primary. The same quantities on
`nll16` (NLL renormalised over the 16 answer symbols) are reported as secondary. CIs come from the
per-document cluster bootstrap: 2000 resamples of documents with replacement, generator seed
20260926, 95% percentile. Every ratio is a ratio of sums over the resampled targets, not a mean of
per-target ratios.

**L — localisation**
- Population: H64, gap 2..M, own slot resident, `own_resample` applied.
- Definition: L = (Σ live_ok − Σ own_resample_ok) / (Σ live_ok − Σ all_slots_zeroed_ok).
- A companion, **L_zero**, uses `own_zero` in the numerator. It is labelled out of distribution: a
  zero key is position-only, and a zero value is the value bias.
- Thresholds:
  - LOCALISED iff the CI lower bound ≥ 0.5.
  - DIFFUSE iff the CI upper bound < 0.5.
  - INDETERMINATE otherwise.
- L is also reported on EXT (secondary) and on the S population.

**S — specificity**
- Population: the L population, restricted to `ctrl_status == 0` and `ctrl_resample` applied.
- Definitions: drop_own = mean(live_ok − own_resample_ok) and drop_ctrl = mean(live_ok −
  ctrl_resample_ok).
- Reported: drop_own, drop_ctrl, drop_own − drop_ctrl, the ratio drop_ctrl / drop_own (each with a
  CI), and the coverage n_S / n_L.
- Thresholds:
  - SPECIFIC iff the ratio's CI upper bound ≤ 0.25.
  - NOT_SPECIFIC iff the ratio's CI lower bound > 0.25.
  - INDETERMINATE otherwise.
  - INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE if coverage < 0.20.
- S is also reported on EXT (secondary).

**Reach — carry-forward past eviction**
- Population: EXT, targets whose own slot is evicted (gap > M, by control 5).
- Definition: excess = mean(live_ok) − mean(baseline_ok), paired per target.
- Bands: 17–20, 21–24, 25–32, 33–40, and the pooled band 17–40 (`max_gap` = 40, so there is no gap
  above 40).
- Baseline is `all_slots_zeroed` (single-step), for three reasons:
  1. It removes exactly what "carried in memory" claims: the memory's content at the query's
     forward. Everything else is left live, including the query's bos-copy context and the trajectory
     that produced the memory.
  2. It is L's denominator, so reach and L use the same intervention.
  3. S0-03's trajectory-wide `slots_zeroed` also changes the bos-copy context and every earlier
     step. Its excess would then include effects of zeroing on the trajectory, which is out of
     distribution for all 48 steps, and not only effects of what the memory holds at the query.
- One consequence to state plainly: information carried by the bos-copy path (the previous
  sentence's gestalt, which sits outside the memory) is in both live and baseline, so it does **not**
  count as carry-forward here.
- The trajectory-wide `slots_zeroed` excess (`answer_readout`, paired) is reported alongside as a
  secondary. So are the same readouts on H64, for continuity with the table above.
- **CARRY(s)** holds iff the pooled 17–40 EXT excess at ckpt 3000 is ≥ REACH_DELTA = 0.03 **and** its
  CI lower bound is > 0. The four bands are descriptive. The pooled band is the decision, which avoids
  choosing the best of 4 × 3 cells after the fact. Band CIs are uncorrected and are labelled so.

## Classification (ckpt 3000)

| condition | result | exit |
|---|---|---|
| any control fails; any measurement raises; any checkpoint missing or mislabelled | `inconclusive` | 3 |
| CARRY on all three seeds | **CARRIED**: answer information survives the eviction of its slot on every seed | 0 |
| CARRY on one or two seeds | **MIXED**: the seeds that carry are named | 0 |
| CARRY on no seed; L LOCALISED on every seed; S NOT_SPECIFIC on no seed | **LOCALISED**: the answer lives in its own slot and is lost with it | 0 |
| CARRY on no seed, otherwise | **UNCLASSIFIED**: the per-seed L and S labels are the result | 0 |

The ckpt-2500 readouts are reported with the same labels, but they are not classified. Whether seed
2's carry is new between 2500 and 3000 is read from them descriptively.

## Prediction (registered; allowed to be wrong)

**MIXED: CARRY on seed 2 only.**
- Seed 2's pooled 17–40 excess at ckpt 3000 is ~0.04–0.08 with its CI clear of 0. Seeds 0 and 1 are
  < 0.03 or have CIs that include 0.
- L is LOCALISED on every seed at both checkpoints, with point values ≥ 0.8.
- S is SPECIFIC on every seed where coverage suffices.
- L_zero is ≥ L. Zero knockout is the harsher, out-of-distribution intervention.
- Second most likely: LOCALISED, if seed 2's H64 excess was a 64-document fluctuation.

## What this does not establish

- Nothing about RSR, ψ̂, eviction policy or Kintsch & van Dijk. FIFO only, and no retention loss.
- No scaling claim.
- A CARRY is a statement about one frozen checkpoint of a model that has not converged. It says
  nothing about the mechanism of carrying. Which later gestalts carry the fact is not measured here.
- The seed-2 carry was seen before this PREREG, so a confirmation is a replication on new documents,
  not a test of a hypothesis blind to it.

**Changes.** Everything not listed here stands as written above.

**A1 (M1): L is like-for-like, and a ratio of sums only**
- **L** (primary) = (Σ live_ok − Σ own_resample_ok) / (Σ live_ok − Σ all_slots_resample_ok).
  `all_slots_resample` puts another document's whole memory at the same step in place of this
  row's, with the queried key excluded.
- **L_zero** (secondary; out of distribution) = (Σ live_ok − Σ own_zero_ok) / (Σ live_ok − Σ
  all_slots_zeroed_ok).
- Both are ratios of sums with the per-document cluster bootstrap. They are never averaged per row.
- The L population is: gap 2..M, `own_status` resident, both `own_resample` and
  `all_slots_resample` applied, and not `dup_key_in_doc` (A3).
- RESAMPLE_MIN_COVERAGE (0.80) now applies to "both resamples applied" among the gap 2..M targets.
- **Denominator guard:** if the 95% CI lower bound of the mean (live_ok − all_slots_resample_ok) on
  the L population is ≤ 0, L is **INDETERMINATE**, whatever its own CI. The memory is then not shown
  to matter within the window, so there is nothing to localise.
- The nll16 analogues stay secondary. So does `live − memory_off` (all slots masked for the query's
  forward), which is reported with its CI as the memory's in-window contribution.

**A2 (M2): the bos-copy context**
- L was already restricted to gap 2..M, so the gap-1 case, where the bos context is the assert's own
  gestalt, is excluded.
- In addition, the `*_bos_off` twin of each primary quantity is reported as a secondary, unlabelled:
  L, S's ratio, and reach excess, each on the same population.

**A3 (M3): answer-object leaks**
The revised instrument excludes donors that carry the answer object. As well as that:
- **The L population excludes** rows with `dup_key_in_doc`. There, the same question is asserted
  twice in the document, so knocking out one slot need not remove the fact.
- **The S population is** the L population, restricted to:
  - `ctrl_status == 0`;
  - `ctrl_resample` applied;
  - not `ctrl_same_object` (a control assert whose object is the queried answer);
  - not `own_donor_same_object` and not `ctrl_donor_same_object` (should be empty after the
    instrument's exclusion; checked and reported).
- S stays paired, own against control on the **same rows**.
- The reported coverage is n_S / n_L. S_MIN_COVERAGE (0.20) applies to it.
- The counts excluded by each rule are reported.

**A4 (M1): the reach baseline is `all_slots_resample`**
- **excess** = mean(live_ok − all_slots_resample_ok), paired per target. The population is EXT,
  `own_status` evicted, gap in band, `all_slots_resample` applied, and not `dup_key_in_doc`.
- **Why this baseline.** It holds the memory in distribution (a real memory at the same fill level)
  and removes only *this document's* content at the query's forward. It is also L's denominator
  after A1.
- **The donor memory may contain the answer object** in an assert of another question
  (`all_donor_has_object`). That is chance-level information a generic memory would carry, so those
  rows are **not** excluded. Their rate is reported.
- **Secondaries, reported with CIs and not classified:**
  - excess over `all_slots_zeroed` (the original A4 baseline);
  - excess over `memory_off`;
  - excess over S0-03's trajectory-wide `slots_zeroed`;
  - the `_bos_off` twin (live_bos_off − all_slots_resample_bos_off).
- **CARRY(s)** is unchanged in form: the pooled 17–40 excess ≥ 0.03 with its CI lower bound > 0, now
  over `all_slots_resample`.
- Resample coverage for reach is reported. **If fewer than 0.80 of the evicted gap 17–40 EXT
  targets of a seed have `all_slots_resample` applied, that seed's CARRY is not decidable, and the
  run is inconclusive (exit 3).**

**A5: the instrument head**
- `loo_readout` is taken from the revised `eng/loo` head that this branch is rebased on. Its sha is
  recorded in the manifest as the run's git sha. That head contains it.
- Controls 3 (bit-exact live) and 5 (residency) carry over:
  - Control 3 now also covers the `*_bos_off` columns of `live` against `answer_readout(cond=

  - Control 5 is now `own_status == resident` iff gap ≤ M, among targets whose assert was written.
