**N-1. The prose kill criterion contradicts the table: "a kill now needs both forms to reject" (v3:363).**

- **Evidence.**

  > "a kill now needs the pct form to reject and raw `AUROC_strat` not to read `AGREE` (raw CI lower < A\*); a raw CI that contains A\* does not block a kill."
- **Alternative (a PM decision, not mine to make).** If "both reject" is what the PM intended, widen 5b to "raw CI **upper ≥** A\*". T12's last case, the M-2 mutation and v3:358 would then change accordingly.
- **Either way:** fix the same phrase if it is carried into any report.
